About SG Han

BFA Industrial Designer in Rhode Island School of Design

I'm SG Han, a designer and creative technologist studying Industrial Design at RISD. I design products where emotion meets logic, combining systems thinking, emerging technology, and human-centered design to create experiences that are intuitive, meaningful, and lasting. I believe great design isn't just about solving problems — it's about creating products people genuinely want to live with.
