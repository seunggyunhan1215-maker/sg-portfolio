5th Armored Brigade
Mascot Design

About Project

Cheolpungi is a mascot design project for the 5th Armored Brigade, blending the brigade's iron-and-wind identity into a friendly, approachable character.

Date:2025. 1. 1 ~ 2026. 1. 2
Type:Graphic Design
Tools:—

Content

鐵 (Iron)
風 (Wind)

Cheolpungi was envisioned by harmonizing the "Cheolpung" (Iron Wind(鐵風)) slogan with the robust silhouette of the K2 howitzer and the dynamic fluidity of the wind.

Details

Headwear: A custom cap designed by capturing the distinctive silhouette and structural lines of the K2 howitzer.
Tactical Uniform: An outfit inspired by the ROK Army's combat uniform, reimagined through a modern and friendly design lens.
Wind Legs: The legs are sculpted to represent the wind, symbolizing the high-mobility of the 5th Armored Brigade.

Additional poses

The core of this mascot design lies in its versatility across various media. From a standard military salute to tactical training and maintenance, Cheolpungi is designed with high scalability to wittily capture the daily lives of service members. Ultimately, it will intuitively communicate the 5th Armored Brigade's dual identity of unyielding strength and approachable friendliness.
